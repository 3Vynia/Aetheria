Seven peoples to be born into, a house that works while you are away, two new tavern games and a calmer screen in **Aetheria**.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://3vynia.github.io/Aetheria/)** or **[on itch.io](https://3vynia.itch.io/aetheria)**.

## New

### Races
- **Seven peoples** marked by the shards of the Crown, chosen when the hero is created:

  | Race | Homeland | Strong at | Trait |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Human | Dawn Meadows | A little more XP in everything | Two extra talent points |
  | Dwarf | Iron Caverns | Mining, smithing, defence | The forge doubles smithing more often |
  | Elf | Shadow Wood | Ranged damage, woodcutting | Mastery grows faster |
  | Bogkin | Rotting Swamp | Healing, farming, herblore | Health comes back twice as fast |
  | Frostborn | Frozen Peaks | Damage reduction, more health | Two more hours of offline progress |
  | Drakeborn | Dragon's Lair | Melee damage, firemaking, cooking | Food never burns |
  | Starborn | Celestial Spire | Magic damage, runecrafting | Omens come more often |

- **One weakness each,** shown next to the strengths, and **three racial talents** per race.
- **Homeland and rival land:** each race fights better against the creatures of its homeland, with more loot, and a little worse in its rival land. Races show on the hero, the map and each region.
- **Rebirth:** the next life can be born as another race, and every race a life is completed with adds 1% XP for good. Saves from before races are human, and their past lives count as human ones.
- **The story knows where you come from:** the first page of the journal, the tavern keeper, the astrologer, the smith and Morwen have words for every people, and completing a life with a race brings back a memory of it.
- **New names every game** for the king and the archmage, like the rest of the people of the story. Games started before keep the names they knew.

### A house that works
- **A floor plan** of all ten rooms. Pick a room to see what it gives now, what the next level adds and what it costs. A gold dot marks the rooms you can build right away.
- **Trophies:** each of the 13 bosses and dungeon lords leaves a trophy once it has fallen enough times. Pay for it in gold and its own spoils, hang it in its room and it gives a small bonus that stays through rebirth.
- **Rooms that work on their own,** also while you are away, each keeping up to twelve hours of work:

  | Room | Makes |
  |---|---|
  | Greenhouse | Your best crop |
  | Royal kitchen | Your most healing dish |
  | Library | XP for your weakest skill |
  | Vault | Interest in gold |

- **Companions at home:** the ones who are neither with the hero nor on an expedition sleep in the house, two per bedroom level. There they gain trust (more with the kitchen), train in the armory and heal sooner.

### Two new tavern games
- **Horseshoes:** three shoes at a stake. Stop one needle for the strength and another for the line; a ringer scores 3, a leaner 2 and a close one 1. Agility slows the needles down.
- **Bards' duel:** a rival minstrel sings a jibe and you have twelve seconds to answer with one of three lines. The crowd cheers a fitting rhyme, chuckles at a lame one and boos one that does not rhyme. Ten verses in each language.

### Tracking board
- **A quiet button beside the hero** shows how much you have on the go and turns green when something is ready to hand in.
- **The board** opens below it, sorted into story, jobs, tavern and tasks with a filter for each, and every row still leads straight to where the objective is done.

### Where your speed comes from
- **The speed tag** of every skill lists each source and what it adds: the house, Agility, prestige, the tool, pets, rebirth, miracles, trophies, class and talents, and in red whatever takes speed away, such as bad weather.

## Changed

- **The company comes along to more fights:** the weekly boss, the tower, omens and festivals as well as bosses and dungeons. Their blows against the weekly boss follow its rules and count towards the attempt. In a region's endless fights they show as waiting.
- **Tavern tables** are grouped into chance, skill and wits, each saying what it is and showing how it has gone for you. Every game is played on a lit felt with a wooden rim.
- **Arm wrestling, rebalanced after simulating it:**
  - every rival comes in a different form each bout;
  - each rung asks for about fifteen more levels of Strength (1, 15, 30, 45, 60, 75 and 90);
  - only about six presses a second count, so Strength matters more than hammering;
  - the weaker rivals only wrestle for a share of the tavern's limit, so beating the farmhand is no longer free gold.
- **Prestige is capped at ten** per skill. A skill at the cap shows *Prestige maxed* instead of the button, and older saves keep any prestige past ten.
- **Tracking** no longer takes a bar above every screen, and the quests screen no longer repeats it.
- **The arena:** both fighters sit at the same height whatever is below them, damage pops over the portrait that takes it (a companion's to one side, a status to the other) and a stunned fighter dims under circling stars.
- **Backups:** the hour and day copies always come from different moments, and the game replaced by importing, restoring or starting a new hero is kept as a fourth copy that can be swapped back.
- **Lighter loading:** each player downloads only the language in use, and the icons are a third smaller.

## Fixed

- **Dates** of backups, past lives and miracles followed the browser's language instead of the game's.
- **Missing icons:** 58 icons were missing from the game's list, so some showed blank.
- **Tracking board headings** let rows show through while scrolling.
