A whole new language, fifty songs in a shuffle and saves that stay yours in **Aetheria**.

**[▶ Play in your browser](https://3vynia.github.io/Aetheria/)** or **[on itch.io](https://3vynia.itch.io/aetheria)**.

## New

### Korean
- **Every text of the game in Korean,** from the journal and the story to the tavern games, the items and the update notes.
- **Fonts made for Hangul** (Gowun Batang and Gowun Dodum), downloaded only by players who pick Korean.
- Pick **한국어** in the language menu, on the title screen or in Settings.

### Fifty songs
- **The music no longer follows the screen.** Fifty themes play in a shuffle, each for two and a half to four minutes, and every one ends on a phrase before a short pause.
- **Twenty new themes,** among them Dawn, Rainfall, Lantern, Highlands, Moonlit, Caravan, Frostfire and Starfall.
- **Fights lift whatever is playing:** any theme with drums plays harder while you fight.
- **Skip:** the sound settings show the song that is on and the next one, with a button to move on.

### Sealed saves
- **Every save is sealed:** the game in the browser, the backups, downloaded files and transfer codes. Outside the game they are unreadable, and a save changed by hand does not open, so nobody can edit a save outside the game.
- **Nothing is lost:** games and backups from earlier versions are sealed as they are the first time they load.
- **Old `.json` backups can still be imported.** One whose numbers no game could reach (play time longer than the save has existed, experience faster than every bonus allows, gold that was never earned) still comes in, marked as altered on the hero. The mark stays through rebirth.
- **Creator saves:** saves made by the creator of the game carry a signature that cannot be forged and show a seal of their own on the hero.
- Saved files now end in `.aetheria`.

### Offline
- **The whole game plays offline** from the first visit, on every screen and in every language.

### Welcome back
- **A new summary** when you return: a clock that fills up to your offline limit, and the gold, experience and creatures counting up.
- **Highlights:** finds, achievements, pets, expeditions, journal pages and more in one list, the rarest first, instead of a burst of pop-ups over the summary. Repeats are folded together (*Rare drop: Bird nest ×146*).
- **Levels climbed** from where they were to where they are, the experience of each skill as bars, and the items brought home and used up.
- **Ultra rare finds** made while away open their own window once the summary is closed.

### Vision
- **Colour-blind friendly:** gains, health and good news in blue, losses and danger in orange (the Okabe-Ito colours), instead of green and red.
- **High contrast:** brighter secondary text and firmer borders, in both themes.
- **Plain letters:** the device's own font instead of the decorative one, titles included.
- A **live preview** beside the switches in *Settings › Accessibility*.

## Changed

- **Sound settings** are built around a live stage that shows what is playing and how each part of the mix sounds.
- **Sound effects** are played on real instruments: bells, harps, glass, wood and more, with a design of their own in each sound pack.
- **Voices** sing on real vowels and consonants, and each character's throat gives them their own timbre.
- **About** credits the maker of the game.

## Fixed

- A tab left open during an update kept failing to load its files offline; it now keeps working.
